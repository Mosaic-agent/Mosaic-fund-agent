### Underlying Mathematical & Control Principles
1. **Zero-Speed High-Frequency Signal Injection (HFI):** At standstill, Back-EMF is zero, preventing standard estimation. SEDEMAC injects high-frequency voltage carriers into the stator windings. By analyzing the high-frequency current response across unequal winding inductances ($L_d \neq L_q$, magnetic saliency), the algorithm resolves rotor position $\theta_e$ instantly at zero speed without roll-back.
2. **Sliding-Mode Observers (SMO):** At nominal operating speeds, non-linear sliding-mode observers reconstruct the rotor velocity and back-EMF vectors with zero phase lag, running at 10 kHz – 20 kHz on low-cost microcontrollers.
3. **Acoustic Noise Shaping:** Cancels torque ripple and eliminates mechanical starter clatter, enabling instantaneous, silent engine start-stop functionality.
