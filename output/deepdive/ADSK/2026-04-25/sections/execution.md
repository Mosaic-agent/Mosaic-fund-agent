## 5. Execution Quality

Autodesk demonstrated an acceleration in topline momentum over the measured three-year horizon, underscoring resilient demand dynamics and robust go-to-market execution. In FY2024, the enterprise generated $5,497M [src: financials[0].revenue_usd_m] in total revenue. This base expanded by 11.5% year-over-year to reach $6,131M [src: financials[1].revenue_usd_m] in FY2025. In the most recent fiscal year, the company’s topline growth profile accelerated further to a 17.5% year-over-year expansion rate, culminating in an absolute revenue figure of $7,206M [src: financials[2].revenue_usd_m] for FY2026. This sequential acceleration in the growth rate indicates a strengthening market position and effective monetization capabilities over the analyzed timeframe.

Operating income scaled concurrently with this revenue expansion, rising from $1,128M [src: financials[0].operating_income_usd_m] in FY2024 to $1,354M [src: financials[1].operating_income_usd_m] in FY2025, before ultimately reaching $1,578M [src: financials[2].operating_income_usd_m] in FY2026. From a margin execution perspective, the corporation exhibited distinct operating leverage in the initial phase, as operating margins expanded notably from 20.5% [src: financials[0].operating_margin_pct] in FY2024 to 22.1% [src: financials[1].operating_margin_pct] in FY2025. However, this trajectory of leverage stalled slightly in the latest reporting period. The operating margin compressed marginally by 20 basis points to 21.9% [src: financials[2].operating_margin_pct] in FY2026, indicating that the accelerated revenue growth required corresponding proportional increases in operating expenditures. Correspondingly, bottom-line profitability progressed from a net income of $906M [src: financials[0].net_income_usd_m] in FY2024 to $1,112M [src: financials[1].net_income_usd_m] in FY2025, before flattening out almost entirely to $1,124M [src: financials[2].net_income_usd_m] in FY2026.

Free cash flow generation reflects the most robust aspect of the company's financial execution, vastly outperforming the modest net income progression. Absolute free cash flow advanced from $1,282M [src: financials[0].free_cash_flow_usd_m] in FY2024 to $1,567M [src: financials[1].free_cash_flow_usd_m] in FY2025, before surging materially to $2,409M [src: financials[2].free_cash_flow_usd_m] in FY2026. Consequently, FCF conversion as a percentage of revenue exhibited a structural and uninterrupted upward trajectory. The company converted 23.3% of its top line to free cash flow in FY2024, improving to a 25.6% conversion rate in FY2025, and ultimately achieving an exceptional 33.4% cash conversion margin in FY2026, pointing to highly efficient working capital management and cash realization dynamics.

From a segment perspective, execution remains highly concentrated within the architecture and construction domains. The AECO division represents the largest absolute revenue contributor to the consolidated entity, delivering $3,583M [src: segments[0].revenue_usd_m] in the most recent period. The legacy AutoCAD and AutoCAD LT segment follows as the second-largest operational pillar, generating $1,787M [src: segments[1].revenue_usd_m]. Additionally, the MFG and M&E segments generated $1,379M [src: segments[2].revenue_usd_m] and $332M [src: segments[3].revenue_usd_m], respectively, while the Other segment accounted for $125M [src: segments[4].revenue_usd_m]. Segment-level year-over-year revenue growth rates are not disclosed [src: segments[0].yoy_growth_pct].

Revenue guidance was not captured in the dataset [src: guidance_revenue_usd_m], precluding a direct comparison of management expectations versus actual fiscal delivery. Similarly, forward earnings per share guidance is not disclosed [src: guidance_eps].

Ultimately, the company is showing consistent delivery characterized by accelerating topline growth and sharply expanding free cash flow conversion, despite a recent plateauing in its core operating margin leverage.
