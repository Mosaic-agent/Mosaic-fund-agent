2 · ETF Premium Trap — Why All International ETFs Are Blocked

NSE-listed international ETFs are subject to SEBI's $7B overseas investment cap. When the cap is breached, fresh unit creation halts, breaking the arbitrage mechanism. ETF prices detach from NAV, trading at 14–20% premiums. Buying at these levels means you need INR to depreciate by that amount just to break even.
