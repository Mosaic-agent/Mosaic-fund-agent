Vector intelligence

Qdrant RAG: semantic memory, not just SQL

Five 768-dimensional collections store embedded representations of every anomaly, fund holding, news article, and portfolio fingerprint. Vector similarity finds cross-asset correlations and historical precedents that SQL can't express.

News RAG · macro correlation

Macro event → price shock correlation

When an anomaly fires, PostMacroShockStrategy embeds the event text and queries news_articles for semantically related headlines in the ±7-day window, no date exact-match required.

Anomaly detected GOLDBEES −5.7% on 2026-05-19 · Flash crash (Z=−4.2)

Engine specs

Every engine, in detail

Signals

6-pillar composite scoring

18+ ETFs scored daily (0–100) combining macro, valuation, sentiment, flows, ML, and volatility.

Anomaly

6-step anomaly engine

MAD-Z, GARCH(1,1), Isolation Forest, PELT, and Volume HMM detect real price shocks & silent block deals.

Attribution

News-RAG attribution

Correlates price moves with earnings leaks, macro shocks, or cross-asset moves via Qdrant similarity.

ML

LightGBM 5-day forecast

Walk-forward CV quantile regression predicting 5-day returns with 80% confidence bands & Kelly sizing.

Risk

Volatility position sizing

GARCH(1,1) inverse-volatility scaling blended 50/50 with Kelly Criterion for optimal capital allocation.

Research

Indian & US equity deep-dives

Parallel research pulling 3-year cashflows, promoter trend checks, QIP dilution audits, and SEC 10-K filings.

Flows

Institutional whale tracking

Tracks monthly portfolio disclosures across 7 multi-asset funds (DSP, Nippon, ICICI, Quant, Bajaj) & CFTC COT.

Alerts

Real-time intraday & iNAV alerts

15-minute iNAV premium/discount arbitrage alerts and live order-flow bid/ask VWAP deviation monitors.

Reports

PDF report export

Generates institutional-grade PDF intelligence reports with GARCH charts, regime tables, and thesis summaries.

Vector

Qdrant semantic memory

5 vector collections storing embedded representations of anomalies, fund holdings, profiles, and news.

Multi-agent system

A guild of specialist analysts

Rather than one LLM with 80 tools, an intent router dispatches to 10 specialist sub-agents, each with a curated tool set, domain system prompt, and hard limits (20 tool calls / 30k tokens / 180s).
